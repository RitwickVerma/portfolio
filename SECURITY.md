# Security Policy

## Supported scope

Only the live site is in scope. Forks, old commits, and local builds are not.

## Reporting a vulnerability

Do not open a public issue. Open a new issue on this project and mark it **confidential**.

Please include:

- A description of the issue and its impact
- Steps to reproduce or a proof of concept
- The affected URL or page

## Bounty and response

No bounty is offered. Reports are handled on a best-effort basis.
