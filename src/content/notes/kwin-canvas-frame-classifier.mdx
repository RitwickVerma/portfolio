## KWin pulls the windows I park out of its reach

kwin-canvas parks windows beyond every monitor on purpose. KWin keeps a window reachable and applies that whenever it looks at a window again, so it moves them closer. The effect has to tell that move from one the window made itself.

## The rule was measured, and a move is judged against it

It was measured in a nested KWin by writing a frame and reading it back. A window with under 100 px of its width over a monitor goes to exactly 100 px: 1840 and 4000 on a 1920 px screen both ended at 1820. A top in the last 28 px goes to the height minus 28. KWin looks again when focus changes, when the work area changes and when the canvas opens or closes.

## One check on the move replaced three guesses at the cause

I had three detectors, each keyed on a cause. They had about twelve call sites and missed whatever the next cause was, my overview opens included. The classifier does not ask why. A move is KWin's when the window kept its size, moved a pixel or more and went from out of reach to in reach. The canvas writes that back and adopts everything else, because only the hand and the canvas move a window.

## A window that insists is adopted, not fought

A rule or script that forces a place would start a fight. A revert followed by another move within 500 ms continues a chain, and the third move of a chain is adopted. After a pause it is a new cause: a quick open, close and open of the canvas reverts all three of KWin's pulls. Try "A window that insists" above.

## The price is that a script cannot drag a far window in

A script that moves an out-of-reach window onto a monitor is undone. To bring a window here, fly to it or drag it.

## The figure runs the real classifier against a stand-in KWin

The three functions are ported to TypeScript from the project's `logic.js` (GPL-2.0-or-later, as the project is) and the tests reuse its unit cases. KWin is not running: its rule here is my reconstruction of the measurements. Why KWin looks at a window again at every open in my own session is not known; the classifier does not need it.
